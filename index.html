Research

Below are a few current and recent directions. This section can be as brief or detailed as you like; feel free to add short bullets, links to preprints, and slide PDFs for quick entry points.

Free energy and kinetic theory from the S‑matrix

with collaborators — thermodynamics from scattering data; transport from correlators

  • Dashen–Ma–Bernstein formalism for interacting gases; connections to Schwinger–Keldysh approaches.
  • Resummations and consistency checks; benchmarking against exactly solvable models.

Holographic QCD and topological defects

baryons, higher‑spin currents, and Chern–Simons sectors

  • Scalar–Chern–Simons toy models illuminating baryon charge and defect spectra.
  • AdS/CFT perspectives on effective string worldsheet dynamics.

Nonequilibrium field theory & wave turbulence

  • Derivation and structure of wave kinetic equations; transport coefficients and hydrodynamic limits.
  • Connections between scattering amplitudes, SK real‑time methods, and kinetic closures.
