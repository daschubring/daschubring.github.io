Daniel A Schubring

Theoretical Physicist • Quantum and Statistical Field Theory

I am a postdoctoral researcher trained in high-energy theory. I apply field-theoretic methods to a variety of systems, from nuclear matter to ordinary surface waves on water. My work focuses on non-equilibrium field theory (wave turbulence, Schwinger–Keldysh methods) and non-perturbative physics (large-N techniques, topological defects, resurgence).
